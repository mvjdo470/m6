## What the Pricing Structure Looks Like in Practice

A few things to know before you pull out your card:

**No monthly billing.** You're committing to at least a year. The good news is the price drops meaningfully if you go two or three years upfront. For email hosting, the annual rate is $29/year; two years runs $49; three years is $59. That's a solid deal if you know you're going to be around for a while.

**Domain registration is separate.** Some plans include a free domain; others don't. Check the specific plan before assuming. SSL certificates are also billed separately — not bundled in by default, which is a bit of a sticking point in 2026 when most hosts include at least a basic SSL for free.

**30-day money-back guarantee.** Annual plan holders who cancel within 90 days are also eligible for a prorated refund, which is a nice extra layer of safety.

No free trial, no freemium tier. You pay to start, but the 30-day guarantee makes the risk minimal.
